**Businesses need a commercial license.** That covers any use by or for a

- employees using MarkDraft for work, on company or personal machines;

- bundling, redistributing or modifying MarkDraft as part of a commercial

## What a commercial license includes

- Use by the licensed number of people for any commercial purpose.
- Updates for the term of the license.
- Email support and priority bug fixes.
- Optional: a license that allows internal modifications and redistribution.

## Not sure whether you need one?

hobby projects, you do not need to pay. If someone pays you, or you use it
as part of your job, you do.
